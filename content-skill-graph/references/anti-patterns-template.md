# Anti-Patterns — Never-Say List
*Know what to never write before writing a single word.*
*For voice rules, see [[voice/brand-voice]]. For what to say instead, see [[engine/hooks]].*

---

## AI Tells (Instant Credibility Killers)

These phrases signal "written by AI" to anyone who reads widely:

- "In today's fast-paced world..."
- "In the ever-evolving landscape of..."
- "Let's dive in." / "Let's explore."
- "Game-changer" / "game-changing"
- "Leverage [anything]" used as a buzzword
- "Unlock the power of..."
- "The future is [already/here/now]..."
- "Transformative" / "transforming [industry]"
- "Cutting-edge" / "state-of-the-art"
- "Revolutionize" / "disrupt"
- "Seamlessly integrate"
- "Synergistically"
- "Holistic approach"
- "At the end of the day..."
- "It goes without saying..."
- "Take your [X] to the next level"

**Rule:** If you've seen it 100 times in AI content, never use it.

---

## Weak Closers

These end posts without landing:

- "What do you think?"
- "Thoughts?"
- "Let me know in the comments!"
- "Which tip was your favorite?"
- "Have you experienced this?"
- Ending with a numbered list item (no coda, no close)
- Ending with a broad statement that could apply to anything

**Replace with:** A specific, answerable question or a conviction statement.

---

## Corporate-Speak

Language that creates distance instead of connection:

- "We are pleased to announce..."
- "I'm excited to share..."
- "It gives me great pleasure..."
- "I had the privilege of..."
- "Honored and humbled to..."
- "Incredibly grateful for..."
- "Passionate about [anything]" used as a descriptor
- "Results-driven" / "data-driven" without data
- "Thought leader" (show it, don't claim it)
- "Industry expert" (same rule)

---

## Vague Abstraction (No Proof)

Statements that sound smart but say nothing:

- "AI is transforming everything."
- "The future belongs to those who adapt."
- "Most companies aren't using AI to its full potential."
- "The key is finding the right balance."
- "Success comes to those who take action."
- Any sentence that could be the end of a motivational poster

**Replace with:** A number, a named tool, a specific moment, a real result.
